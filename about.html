ABOUT UMAR

The journey behind the writing.

Born in Pakistan, Umar Rafi has lived in Iraq, Canada, the United States, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Lebanon and Malaysia. Moving between these countries has meant encountering different ways of living, believing and understanding the world. His journey has crossed aviation, technology, finance and entrepreneurship, each bringing its own perspective on people and the institutions they build.

Those perspectives have grown through friendships as much as through work. Across borders often defined by conflict and competing narratives, Umar has formed lifelong friendships with people of different nationalities, religions and backgrounds. The warmth of those individual relationships, set against the divisions between communities, lies behind a recurring question in his fiction: why do compassionate, well-meaning people so often find themselves on opposing sides?

Literature gives him room to explore that question through the lives of his characters: their loves, loyalties, ambitions and the choices they face. A Children’s Playground draws on the journeys and encounters that have shaped his outlook, bringing that curiosity into a story of love, duty and becoming.

His professional life has also found its way into his books. Antifragility of Islamic Finance examines risk-sharing and the resilience of financial systems; Banking 3.0 explores the architecture required as artificial intelligence moves closer to action in banking. These subjects meet his fiction in a shared interest in how individual choices and larger systems shape one another, though literature remains the form closest to his heart.

Poetry offers another space for that interest in human lives. Alongside his own verse, Umar translates poetry from Urdu and his native Punjabi into English. The translations bring voices he cherishes to new readers, extending the connections across languages and cultures that have marked his own journey.

Umar holds a B.S. in applied mathematics from the United States Air Force Academy, an M.S. in computer science from Wichita State University, a Ph.D. in finance from INCEIF, and an M.S. in machine learning from Georgia Tech.
