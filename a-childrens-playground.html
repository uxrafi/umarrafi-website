ABOUT THE NOVEL

A life divided.
A heart caught between.

An alternate contemporary setting, against the backdrop of the Global War on Terror.

A Children’s Playground is a coming-of-age novel set against the backdrop of the Global War on Terror, exploring how love, loss, duty, and war shape the moral journey of one young man. Spanning continents, cultures, and religions in an alternate contemporary setting, it examines the timeless themes of class, family, faith, identity, and fate, asking what it truly means to become the person one is destined to be.

Born into a prominent tribal family along the Pakistan–Afghanistan border, Asad dreams of escaping the expectations of his lineage to build a life in Silicon Valley rather than succeeding his aging father as tribal chief. His carefully planned future is transformed when he meets Anna, a gifted musician and artist at Stanford University whose optimism, curiosity, and free spirit challenge everything he believes about love, happiness, and himself.

As their relationship deepens, tragedy strikes both worlds Asad calls home. A devastating drone strike in his remote mountain village leaves his childhood friend, Samina, critically injured and forces him to confront the human cost of a conflict he has spent years trying to leave behind. Torn between the life he longs to build in America and the responsibilities he has inherited in Pakistan, Asad embarks on a journey that will ultimately cost him the woman he loves—not once, but twice.

Stretching from the tribal mountains of the northwestern Pakistan–Afghanistan border to the heart of Silicon Valley, A Children’s Playground is ultimately the story of a boy becoming a man—discovering that the greatest battles are often fought not between nations, but within the human heart, where love and duty, ambition and sacrifice, hope and grief struggle to coexist.
