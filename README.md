# Umar Rafi — Author Website

Official source repository for **umarrafi.com**.

The site is being designed as a long-term author platform for books, essays, poetry, reflections, media, and future literary work.

## Planned structure

- Home
- Books
- Writing
  - Essays
  - Poetry
  - Reflections
- About
- Media & Speaking
- Contact

## Design direction

Literary, elegant, international, thoughtful, and understated. The author identity is primary; technology and finance are part of the body of work rather than the visual identity of the site.

## Status

Initial design/build in progress.
