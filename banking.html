Technologist

Banking technology.

Twenty-five years. Three eras of banking.

For close to twenty-five years, I have helped shape the software that banking depends on, developing expertise in financial systems and architecture. My work spans banking data, risk and regulatory systems, cloud-based financial-services platforms and artificial intelligence. It is grounded in building solutions for institutions ranging from microfinance providers in developing countries to some of the world’s largest banks, across North America, Europe and the Middle East.

I have worked through three phases of banking technology: systems that record, platforms that connect and AI agents that act. Throughout that evolution, banking has remained my domain, giving me a perspective on how each generation inherits the strengths and limitations of the last. Today, I bring that knowledge to autonomous AI, defining the architecture, governance and controls that allow intelligence to act within clear boundaries of authority, evidence and accountability.

Oracle · 2007–2015

FRS Global · 2005–2007

I led Canadian operations across products and services, overseeing the design and implementation of an OSFI-compliant Basel II reporting software solution for the Canadian market: Pillar I (credit, market and operational risk), Pillar II (Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process, or ICAAP), and Pillar III (public disclosure).

Verax · 2003–2005

I established Verax’s software-development lifecycle (SDLC) practice, leading the adoption and implementation of the IBM Rational Suite for requirements management, change and configuration management, and quality assurance.

Banking 2.0

Platforms that connect

As a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect (CTA), I served as Chief Architect on multi-cloud engagements in the fintech vertical. I set up and led the startup’s Software Engineering department. My team designed its flagship product to automate the sales and underwriting lifecycles of life and health insurance policies on a hosted application service provider (ASP) platform.
