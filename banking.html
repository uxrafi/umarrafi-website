Banking technology.

Twenty-five years. Three eras of banking.

For more than twenty-five years I have worked where banking meets software: in the data, risk and regulatory systems of large institutions, in the cloud platforms that connected them to their customers, and now in the governance of AI agents that can act on their behalf. The work has taken me from microfinance institutions in developing countries to some of the largest banks in North America and Europe, and across the Middle East, South Asia and Africa.

In every era the same questions return: where the data lives, who may change it, how a decision is evidenced and how a regulator is satisfied. Each generation of technology answers them differently. I have helped banks answer them through each generation, and that history is the best preparation I know for what comes next.

Three eras of banking

1.0

Banking 1.0: Systems that record

The bank’s institutional memory: data, risk and regulatory systems running inside the institution.

  • FRS Global 2005–2007

    I built the Canadian operation from inception. Within eighteen months our clients included four of Canada’s six largest banks, with regulatory reporting and Basel II across credit, market and operational risk.

  • Oracle

    I led the Risk and Compliance practice across the Middle East and Africa, bringing risk, regulatory and compliance systems to banks across the region.

  • Verax 2003–2005

    I established the software-development lifecycle practice for dependable enterprise systems.

  • Ardent Software 1998–2000

    Data warehousing with DataStage: moving and preparing the enterprise data every banking application depends on.

2.0

Banking 2.0: Platforms that connect

Cloud, software as a service and digital banking: data and services brought together across channels.

  • Salesforce Eight years

    I helped shape Financial Services Cloud and architected cloud transformations for banks and wealth managers across North America and Europe, as a Certified Technical Architect.

  • Varo Bank 2016–2017

    On the founding technology leadership team of a digital bank, I worked across core banking, governance, compliance, integration and regulatory controls.

  • WorldInsure and Catapulse 2000–2002

    Early software as a service: hosted insurance and hosted development platforms, at the very start of cloud software.

3.0

Banking 3.0: Agents that act

Intelligence that carries work within explicit boundaries of authority, evidence and accountability.

  • Sayaa.ai Today

    I co-founded Sayaa.ai to bring runtime assurance to autonomous financial systems: discovering, controlling and demonstrating how AI agents behave.

  • Tessera Labs 2025–2026

    As VP of Enterprise Architecture and Delivery, I led architecture and delivery for the Mosaic AI-agent platform.

  • Salesforce

    I architected AI-agent solutions for financial institutions.

  • Georgia Tech Research

    My research examines the runtime governance of enterprise AI-agent workflows.

  • Banking 3.0 December 2026

    My book on the architecture banks need as intelligence learns to act.
