A life across worlds.

Umar Rafi’s life has been an accidental journey across countries, cultures and professions. Born in Pakistan, he has lived in Iraq, Canada, USA, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Lebanon and Malaysia. His work in entrepreneurship, aviation, technology and finance, and the friendships he has made along the way, have shaped his curiosity about people and what drives them.

His studies and career led him to write books on technology and finance, but the writing closest to his heart is fiction. His novels draw on that learning and lived experience to follow characters across cultures and borders as they navigate love, loyalty and duty, exploring the choices that bring people together and the beliefs that set them apart.

Beyond the writing

Professional background

My professional journey began at the United States Air Force Academy, where I earned a B.S. in applied mathematics. I briefly served as a fighter pilot before moving into technology and finance, carrying with me an appreciation for preparation, responsibility and the consequences of decisions. Aviation has remained part of my life: I am a licensed pilot and a skydiving coach.

Over more than twenty-five years, I have worked across enterprise software, banking and entrepreneurship. Early engineering and architecture roles at Ardent Software, Catapulse, WorldInsure and Verax led to building FRS Global’s Canadian operations and leading Oracle’s Risk and Compliance practice across the Middle East and Africa. At Varo, I joined the founding technology leadership team, helping build the foundations of a digital bank. During eight years at Salesforce, I helped shape Financial Services Cloud and architected AI-agent solutions for financial institutions. I later led Enterprise Architecture and Delivery at Tessera Labs. Today, as founder of Sayaa.ai, I am working on runtime assurance for autonomous financial systems, exploring how their actions can remain within defined responsibilities and controls.

Alongside my career, I earned an M.S. in computer science from Wichita State University, a Ph.D. in finance from INCEIF and an M.S. in machine learning from Georgia Tech. I am also a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect. My doctoral research with Abbas Mirakhor examined risk-sharing, equity-based finance and antifragility. Teaching has offered another way to connect that learning with practice, through appointments at UC Berkeley Extension, SP Jain School of Global Management and Hamdan Bin Mohammed Smart University, as well as computer science teaching and mentorship through iCodeGuru.

Education

These strands meet in my writing. Antifragility of Islamic Finance draws on my research into financial resilience, while Banking 3.0 explores the architecture required as intelligence moves closer to action. Both reflect questions that have followed me through my professional life: how systems shape our choices, and how we respond when circumstances change. Fiction remains closest to my heart, giving those questions a human scale through the lives, relationships and decisions of individual characters.
