ABOUT UMAR

A life across worlds.

Umar Rafi is an author, technologist and entrepreneur whose life has been an accidental journey across countries, cultures and professions. From aviation to banking and enterprise technology, his path has brought different ways of seeing the world. That curiosity finds its fullest expression in literature, where he explores the relationships, beliefs and choices that shape our lives—and the understanding that can grow when we see a familiar question from another side.

His books span literature, technology and finance, connected by an interest in how people and the systems they build respond to change and uncertainty. Literature remains closest to his heart, giving those questions room to unfold through stories and characters.

Poetry offers a more intimate voice. Alongside his own verse, Umar translates poetry from Urdu and his native Punjabi into English, sharing voices he cherishes with readers across languages and cultures.
